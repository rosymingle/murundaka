*We acknowledge and respect the traditional owners of the land on which we are privileged to live.*

*Murundaka* is a local Wurundjeri word meaning 'a place to stay or live.'

Murundaka is an all-rental, housing co-operative based in Heidelberg Heights, Melbourne, Australia. We are members of Earth Common Equity Rental Cooperative and a proud member of the Common Equity Housing program. Murundaka was established in 2011. We are 20 households of 35-40 people ranging in age from toddlers to elders.

We live as a community, sharing space, responsibilities, meals, hardship and joy.
